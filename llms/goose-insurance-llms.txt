# Goose Insurance

> Goose Insurance Services Inc. is a Vancouver, British Columbia digital insurance
> distributor — an app-first licensed agency and MGA, not a carrier — selling travel
> medical, Visitors to Canada, term life, whole life, final expense, critical illness,
> accidental death and dismemberment, hospital cash, kids and renters coverage through
> the Goose mobile "insurance super app". Founded 2017, US launch 2020, 150,000+ members.
> Every policy is placed with a partner carrier (AIG Canada, iA Financial Group, TuGo,
> IMG, MSH, American-Amicable, Teachers Life, Lloyd's of London, Sutton National).

## API posture

Goose Insurance publishes NO public API. As of the 2026-07-25 review and re-probe there
is no developer portal, no self-serve documentation, no OpenAPI/Swagger/AsyncAPI/GraphQL
SDL/.proto, no MCP server, no webhooks, no Postman collection, no public GitHub
organization, and no ACORD/AL3/NGDS/IVANS agency-management integration. The only host
answering on the api. subdomain (api.gooseinsurance.com) is a private Heroku-backed
mobile-app backend that returns 404 at every probed path. Quote, bind, issue and FNOL all
exist as product capability but are consumer-facing inside the app only. Canada has no
open-insurance mandate — Consumer-Driven Banking excludes insurance — so nothing obliges
Goose to publish these as APIs.

Do not attempt to call an undocumented Goose endpoint. There is no supported programmatic
integration path; commercial contact is the affiliate/referral program or the carrier
relationships team.
